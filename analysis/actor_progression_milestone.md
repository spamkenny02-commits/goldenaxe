# v0101 - original conditional actor loading 1805..1838

The previous loader preserved every surviving actor from the room list regardless of the dungeon progression record. Original 1805 ORs the display flags of the first eight surviving records after applying persistence. If bit 40 is present and byte C0CE plus the low byte of C037 is nonzero, it clears those eight records. A progression byte with bit 7 clear then inserts type 0F in slot zero; bit 7 set leaves them empty. This runs before actor counting and unique pattern allocation.

The native loader now follows that order. A new progression-aware entry takes the byte explicitly; the existing zero-progression entry remains for standalone callers. All four production room-loading paths use load_current_world_actors, which reads the original-layout byte at game_save_state[1E + (u8)world_dungeon_number] and supplies the saved room mask. Existing graphics resource data and subsequent actor behavior are unchanged.

A separate SMS fixture calls original 1780..18E1 unchanged, including the conditional block, pattern decompression, remapping and VDP writes. A separate compiled MD harness includes production main.c and calls its actual loader wrapper. The 2248 cases cover every one of 512 room lists with full persistence and progression 00,01,80,FF. The ten boss-flag room lists additionally exercise persistence masks 00,A5,80,01 and progression 00,01,7F,80,FF. Selected progression indices vary, including FF, with a deliberately different adjacent byte. All 768 actor-record bytes, active count, allocated tile count and loaded planar pattern bytes match the original for every case.

The scope audit constrains modifications to actor loading, its declaration and four production caller replacements. Ordinary movement, three exterior scroll directions and eight deterministic enemy motions are rechecked in PAL/NTSC, along with the controlled forest-maze sequence and its final room maps. Inventory returns and dungeon entry/exit regression results are recorded separately.

This implements the conditional loader, not complete dungeon victory or quest progression. Natural boss fights, writing/collecting the progression object, its behavior and rendered sprites, complete save/continue progression, full playthrough, audio and exact global loader timing remain unverified or unfinished. Existing CPU/VDP phase discrepancies remain. These controlled comparisons cannot establish that the entire game is ported.
